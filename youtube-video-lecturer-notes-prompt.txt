The "Technically Exhaustive" Prompt
"I need you to process this video/transcript into a Lecture Script or Textbook Chapter format.

Core Requirement: Do NOT summarize, trim, or compress the content. I am a beginner and need every detail to understand 
the context.

Specific Instructions:

Exhaustiveness: Include every single example, side-note, specific number, file path, and book title mentioned by 
                the instructor. If he repeats a concept for emphasis, ensure the explanation in the notes is equally 
                detailed.

Narrative Style: Do not just use bullet points. Use full sentences and paragraphs to explain the 'Why' and 'How' 
                 (e.g., tell the story of why we need Subnetting, don't just list the steps).

Technical Validation: If the instructor makes a mistake (like writing the wrong number on the whiteboard but explaining 
                      the correct logic), capture his explanation but add a correction note so I learn the technically 
                      correct info.
                      
Zero Loss: Treat this as a legal transcription where every piece of information is vital.

The final output should look like a thorough University Lecture Note."
